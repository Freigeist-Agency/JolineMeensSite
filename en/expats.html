A new country brings many opportunities

— but also many challenges. Especially when it comes to finances, real estate and long-term decisions. This is exactly where I come in: I support international clients who want to find a home in Germany or build up their assets in a targeted manner — with comprehensible, personal and bilingual advice.

As an experienced investment advisor based in the Aachen region, I have focused on holistic financial planning for expats specialized — with a particular focus on Construction financing.

What I can do for you:

Advice in English or German — whichever you prefer

Accompaniment to important appointments — e.g. bank, notary, broker

Comprehensible support through the financial jungle — no technical jargon

Holistic financial planning — including insurance, wealth creation & retirement planning

Translation & explanation of documents — from financing agreement to self-disclosure

real estate advice — including realistic financing options & funding opportunities

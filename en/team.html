A family business with heart and experience

My parents, Frank and Sandra Meens, have been active in the financial services industry for over 25 years. It was especially my father who inspired me to support people on their journey to homeownership – a path often shaped by big dreams and even bigger decisions.

For over three years now, I’ve been part of our family business – helping people turn their dream of owning a home into reality. Together with my brother, we give our best every day to shape a company that is modern, innovative, and future-oriented.

Our Team

Throughout my professional journey, I quickly realized that people don’t just dream of owning a home – many are just as focused on their careers and future prospects. That’s why I’ve taken on a leadership role, guiding a small, motivated team of individuals who want more out of life.

Whether they’re experienced professionals, career changers, or just starting out – my goal is to offer each person the right training and development opportunities. Because we believe that personal growth and professional success go hand in hand.

Together, we’re building an environment where talent is nurtured, potential is recognized, and everyone has the chance to become part of our family business – with the shared mission of helping even more people achieve their goals and dreams.

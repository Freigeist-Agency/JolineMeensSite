Home Financing with Vision

So that your dream home also becomes a good home!

For many people, the journey to owning their own home is one of the most important steps in life — both emotionally and financially. As a home financing consultant, I support my clients on exactly this journey — with advice that  ends with figures, but starts with quality of life.

I attach great importance to ensuring that my customers feel financially comfortable even after moving in: with enough monthly leeway to breathe deeply, for the family, for life. A solid calculation is therefore not only a duty for me, but a matter close to my heart.

An important part of my advice is the integration of state funding — from KfW funding to NRW Bank programs and BAFA subsidies. These funding opportunities are often the decisive building block for making dreams come true faster and more confidently — and unfortunately they are overlooked far too often.

Even if the right property has not yet been found, it is worthwhile to calculate together at an early stage: What is realistic, what is sustainable — and what feels good? This preliminary calculation provides clarity and security in a competitive market. And sometimes even my network helps you find the right property — whether through brokerage contacts, private recommendations or cooperation with property developers.

My goal is not just to arrange financing — but to help you create a home that really suits you.
